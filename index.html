Know the data before using it

The source file is named “Global GDP Explorer 2025”, but its GDP and population column headings specify 2023. The explorer therefore labels this a 2023 snapshot. The repository attributes it to World Bank / UN data; a direct upstream extract and revision date are not supplied.

Quality checks and interpretation

GDP is nominal, in US dollars. Per-capita GDP is an average, not household income. Reported growth is retained as supplied; its precise price basis is not documented. World-share percentages are supplied and rounded, rather than recomputed from an incomplete country selection. Dataset sum and selected-country share always use the checked-in rows as their denominator.
